#### Faster dependency discovery in large repositories

`run --all`, and `find` or `list` with `--dependencies`, are faster in large repositories, including ones with no `dependency` blocks. The time spent linking units to their dependencies now grows linearly with the number of units instead of quadratically.

In [benchmarks](https://github.com/gruntwork-io/terragrunt/blob/main/internal/discovery/benchmark_relationship_test.go) on an AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395, discovering units and their dependencies in a generated repository of 10-unit dependency chains:

| units | before | after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | 0.63ms | 0.63ms |
| 100 | 5.2ms | 5.0ms |
| 1,000 | 56ms | 47ms |
| 10,000 | 1.32s | 443ms |

At 10,000 units, memory allocated during discovery dropped from 8.6 GB to 1.9 GB.
