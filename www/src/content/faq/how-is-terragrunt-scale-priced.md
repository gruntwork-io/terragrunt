[Terragrunt Scale](/terragrunt-scale) offers unlimited runs and unlimited resources, and there is a free tier. The Free and Team tiers are limited by the number of Terragrunt [Units](https://docs.terragrunt.com/features/units/) being deployed, with no usage meter, and Enterprises are offered custom pricing. Terragrunt Scale has no resources-under-management (RUM) pricing: you are never charged based on the number of resources you manage.
